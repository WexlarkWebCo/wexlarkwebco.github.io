Questions and answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with businesses outside your local area?

Yes. BrightPath Web Design works remotely with small businesses across the United States.

How much does a website cost?

Our listed website packages start at $499, $899, and $1,499. The final price depends on the features, content, and scope agreed upon for your project.

How long does a website take to build?

Timing depends on the size of the project, the features requested, and how quickly the necessary content and feedback are provided. We'll discuss an estimated timeline before work begins.

Can you update my website after it launches?

Yes. Optional monthly maintenance plans are available for routine website updates within the agreed plan limits.

Do I need to provide photos and written content?

We'll discuss what you already have and what is needed for your website. Any stock imagery, supplied materials, or additional content requirements will be discussed as part of the project.

Does a website guarantee more customers?

No website can guarantee sales, search rankings, or a specific number of leads. A well-organized website can help present your business professionally and make it easier for potential customers to contact you.
