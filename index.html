Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Here are a few things business owners commonly want to know before starting a website project.

How much does a website cost?

Our website packages start at $499, with Business at $899 and Premium at $1,499. The right package depends on your website's size, features, and project requirements.

How long does it take to build a website?

Timing depends on the size of the project, the features needed, and how quickly content and feedback are provided. We'll discuss a realistic timeline before the project begins.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Our website designs use responsive layouts intended to display properly on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

Can I request changes to my website?

Absolutely. Project details and revision expectations will be discussed before work begins so everyone understands what is included.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes. Optional monthly maintenance plans are available for routine website updates. Each plan includes a set amount of update time per month.

Do I need to know anything about building websites?

No. You can tell us about your business and what you want your website to accomplish. We'll help explain the design and project details along the way.

How do I get started?

Send us an inquiry using the contact form below. Tell us about your business and what you're looking for, and we'll take it from there.
