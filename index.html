Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Send an inquiry using the contact form. We'll discuss your business, website goals, and the package that fits your needs.

When do I pay the deposit?

A 50% deposit is due before work begins. The remaining 50% is due before the completed website is launched.

Can I request changes?

Project review and agreed revisions will be discussed as part of your project scope. Additional work outside that scope will be quoted separately.

Do I have to purchase maintenance?

No. Monthly maintenance is optional and can be selected if you want ongoing update assistance.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on the size of the project, the agreed scope, and how quickly content and feedback are provided. We'll discuss an estimated timeline before work begins.
