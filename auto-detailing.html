Common questions

Questions About Getting a Website

Do I need to provide photos of my detailing work?

Your own vehicle and detailing photos are ideal for a gallery. We'll discuss the content and images needed for your project.

Can my website show my detailing packages?

Yes. Your website can list services, package descriptions, starting prices if you choose to publish them, and ways to request a custom quote.

Can customers request a quote through my website?

We can include contact and quote-request options. The exact setup will be confirmed in your project scope.

Do you work with businesses outside your local area?

Yes. BrightPath works with small businesses remotely throughout the United States.

Do I have to sign up for monthly maintenance?

No. Monthly maintenance is optional. Any ongoing services and their terms will be discussed separately.
