Frequently asked questions

Good to Know Before You Book.

A few helpful answers to common questions about vehicle detailing.

How long does a detailing appointment take?

Timing depends on vehicle size, condition, and the services selected. A provider should give you an estimated timeframe after reviewing the job.

How much does auto detailing cost?

Pricing depends on the vehicle, its condition, and the level of service. The example packages above provide a starting point for this sample website.

What's the difference between a car wash and detailing?

A car wash generally focuses on routine exterior cleaning. Detailing involves more thorough cleaning and attention to interior and exterior surfaces.

What is paint correction?

Paint correction uses specialized polishing techniques to reduce the appearance of certain paint imperfections. The appropriate process depends on the paint's condition.

What does ceramic coating do?

Ceramic coatings create a protective layer on suitable vehicle surfaces. Performance and durability vary by product, preparation, and maintenance.

Do prices change for larger vehicles?

They can. SUVs, trucks, vans, and heavily soiled vehicles may require additional time and materials. A vehicle-specific estimate helps clarify the cost.
