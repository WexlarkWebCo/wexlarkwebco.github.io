Good questions

Before We Get to Work

Here are a few things homeowners commonly want to know before starting a project.

Do you provide free estimates?

This sample business offers an initial project discussion. Whether an in-person estimate is free would depend on the actual contractor's policies and the job.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Typical handyman work may include minor repairs, painting, furniture assembly, hardware installation, and small home improvements. Actual services depend on qualifications and local requirements.

How do I know what my project will cost?

Share the details of your project first. A contractor can review the scope, materials, labor, and any other factors before providing an estimate.

Do I need to supply the materials?

That can vary by project. Material responsibilities and costs should be discussed and agreed upon before work begins.

Can I ask about more than one repair?

Absolutely. Listing multiple jobs in your initial message can help the contractor understand the full scope of the work.

Do you handle electrical or plumbing work?

Some work may require a properly licensed specialist. A contractor should confirm qualifications and local requirements before accepting specialized work.
